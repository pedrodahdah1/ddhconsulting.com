How it works

What happens, step by step.

Every step has a clear end, and you decide whether to go on.

01

The free Reporting Review

You send the form. We reply the same day and set a 30-minute call. On the call we go through your close, your cash and your reports. Then you get a written assessment within 1 business day after the call.

More detail

The assessment covers: the reports you produce today and how long each takes; where each number comes from; where numbers disagree; the first three fixes we would make; a rough effort for each; what a setup would include. You receive that written assessment within 1 business day after the call.

02

Setup

We agree the scope and the fee before we start. We build the pipelines, the model, the reports and the close routine in your environment, with test rounds until you accept it.

More detail

We write the run book as we build. Typical durations for a setup.

03

The monthly retainer

Each month the model refreshes, the close is finished, and the forecast, cash and payer view are updated. You hear right away if a file is late.

More detail

Minimum six months, then month to month. You can ask for the handover at any time: the run book, training and access for your team.

04

What we need from you

  • Access to exports from the EHR or practice management system, billing, the bank and accounting
  • One person on your side who owns the numbers with us
  • Your current reports, even if they are spreadsheets
  • A short check-in each month

05

How we handle data

We build inside your Microsoft tenant and your existing tools. By default we work with aggregated numbers. Patient-level data is used only with a signed BAA, a clear need and an eligible service. Our setup is HIPAA-eligible with a signed BAA and the correct configuration. Forms on this site go through Formsubmit.co. Bookings go through Calendly. See Privacy.

06

What we don't do

  • We don't replace your biller or your CPA. We work alongside them and connect to the systems they already use.
  • We don't lock you in. You own the model, the reports and the run book. After six months you can leave or take it in-house.
  • We don't sell or share your data. It is used only to build and run your reporting.
  • We don't take on work we can't run every month. If a project is a one-off, we will say so on the call.
