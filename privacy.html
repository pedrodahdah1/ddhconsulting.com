Privacy

What the forms on this site collect, and how we use it.

Reporting Review

If you request a free Reporting Review, the form collects your name, work email, role, organization type, locations or providers, the systems you check, whether you have a BI team, whether your billing company already sends a full monthly reporting package, and what takes the most time in reporting.

Reporting Health Check

You can take the Health Check without sending anything. Your answers stay in this browser. If you choose to send the results, the form collects your name, organization, work email, your score, the gaps flagged, and your answers.

How we use it

We use this information to reply to you and to prepare for a conversation. Messages go to contact@ddhbiconsulting.com. We do not sell this information.

Please do not include patient information in either form.

Analytics

The site loads Google Analytics, which may store a cookie in your browser so we can see which pages are visited.
