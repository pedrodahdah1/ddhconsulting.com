Privacy

What this site collects, who receives it, and how we use it.

Who receives the forms

Both forms on this site are sent to Formsubmit.co, which delivers the message to contact@ddhbiconsulting.com. We use what you send to reply to you and to prepare for a conversation. We do not sell this information.

Reporting Review

If you request a free Reporting Review, the form collects your name, work email, organization name, and what you want to fix first. You can also share your role, organization type, locations or providers, and the systems you already use.

Reporting Health Check

You can take the Health Check without sending anything. Your answers stay in this browser. If you choose to send the results, the form collects your name, organization, work email, your score, the gaps flagged, and your answers. That message also goes through Formsubmit.co.

Booking

You can book a call on Calendly from this site. The booking link leaves this site. Calendly receives what you enter on their page.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics, measurement ID G-J5QVLV1G6C. After the page loads, the browser requests Google's script. Google Analytics may set a cookie. It records the page you viewed, and the events we send when you start or finish the Health Check, send a form, or click the booking link. We look at pages and those events to see what gets used. The form email is a separate message. You can block the cookie in your browser.

Contact

Questions about this page: contact@ddhbiconsulting.com.

Please do not include patient information in either form.
