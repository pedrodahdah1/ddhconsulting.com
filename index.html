What the work looks like

What DDH delivers

DDH provides fractional finance and data leadership for healthcare operators, working in the client's own accounts.

Each month, clients receive:

  • Month-end close. The P&L is tied to the general ledger after close, with the variance explained.
  • Rolling forecast. The budget, the rolling forecast, and the variance, kept current through the year.
  • Cash and runway. Cash on hand, collections, and runway in one view.
  • Payer economics. Remittances matched to the ledger, including denials, underpayments, and billing and AR leakage.
  • Board pack. The monthly pack and the board deck, from the same definitions.

Power BI is the monthly reporting system: we keep the data current and produce the reports after close, as part of the finance-lead work. The scheduled AI briefing is sent from that same model, at a time the client sets.

Managed Financial Reporting

The monthly retainer is a weekly commitment with a fixed list of deliverables, all run in the client's accounts: the close, the four leadership views, Power BI reporting, and the scheduled AI briefing.

Inside the retainer, we build Month-End Autopilot, Cash Truth, the Commission Engine, and BI Modernization. After a six-month minimum, the client can continue month to month, or take a handover: the run book, the training, and their team runs it from there.

Scheduled AI briefing

On a schedule the client sets, a job reads the KPIs from their Power BI model and drafts a short email, sent from their Outlook. We build it in their environment, work through the numbers and the wording with them, and leave it running there. They can take just the briefing, or include it in the monthly retainer. Once it is live, it is a scheduled job, not a chat with an assistant.

The briefing runs in the client's Microsoft environment, under HIPAA, their contracts, and the rules of their tenant.

The standard briefing uses aggregated indicators only. The design is HIPAA-eligible with a signed BAA and the correct configuration.

There are two ways to deliver it: everything stays in the client's Microsoft tenant, or we use an enterprise API with a compliance filter. How Path A and Path B work.

Who it's for

This is for you if you run an independent or specialty pharmacy, a physician practice, an ASC, an MSO, or another healthcare operator, and you need a finance lead for the close, the forecast, cash, payer economics, and the board pack. If you already have a BI team, that still works: we work with them.

It is probably not for you if you want a one-off dashboard with no one running it after it goes live, or if nobody on your side can own the numbers with us.

We typically work with CFOs, COOs, CEOs, and practice administrators.

Method

We use the Cross-Check method on every engagement. Delivery is on Power BI, Power Automate, Copilot Studio, Microsoft Fabric, and SQL/Azure. Data sources typically include Excel, QuickBooks, and NetSuite. The client owns the code. Most financial reporting uses aggregated indicators. When patient-level data is required, we sign a BAA first and use a design that is HIPAA-eligible with a signed BAA and the correct configuration.
