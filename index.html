Revenue Intelligence

Six categories. Every dollar tracked.

Healthcare organizations lose an estimated 5–15% of earned revenue to systematic leakage across multiple operational touchpoints. DDH's revenue intelligence platform integrates your clinical, operational, and financial data to identify, quantify, and monitor leakage across six core categories — giving your finance leadership a real-time, data-driven view of revenue performance across your entire payer mix.

01

Claims & Denials Management

Track denial rates, root causes, and recovery performance by payer, service line, and provider. Identify patterns before they compound.

02

Contractual Underpayments

Cross-reference actual payments against contracted rates to surface systematic underpayments and quantify exposure by payer.

03

Authorization & Eligibility Gaps

Identify services rendered without authorization and eligibility failures creating write-off exposure before they become bad debt.

04

Charge Capture Integrity

Detect charge capture failures and documentation gaps between clinical activity and billing records — recovering revenue before the billing window closes.

05

Program Revenue Optimization

Maximize revenue from managed care programs, quality incentive structures, and specialty drug dispensing margins through data-driven program management.

06

Payer Contract Variance

Monitor contract performance over time and surface variance trends before they compound into material revenue losses at contract renewal.

Leakage categories are customized to your organization's payer mix, service lines, and operational environment during the assessment phase.
