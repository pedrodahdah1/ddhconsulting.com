Where Reporting Breaks

If any of this sounds familiar, we should talk.

Most healthcare businesses we talk to aren't short on data. They have a pharmacy or practice-management system, an accounting system, bank portals, and payroll. The problem is that none of it connects, so someone stitches it together by hand every month.

Month-end

Closing the books takes weeks

The same exports get pulled, pasted, and fixed in the same workbook every month. One late file and everything slips.

What we build: automated refreshes that pull the data and produce the monthly package on schedule.
Reconciliation

Deposits, remits, and the GL don't agree

Cash hits the bank, payers send remittances, and the ledger shows something else. The difference gets plugged instead of explained.

What we build: a reconciliation that matches each layer and lists the exceptions with a reason.
Commissions

Pay calculations get disputed every cycle

Sales commissions or provider incentives are rebuilt by hand, so every payout invites questions about how the number was reached.

What we build: a documented calculation with a statement each person can check.
Visibility

Simple questions take days to answer

Which location is down this month? Which payer is slowest to pay? The data exists, but getting the answer means another spreadsheet.

What we build: dashboards by location, payer, provider, or product line, ready when you ask.
Key person

Only one person knows how the reports work

The process lives in one workbook and one person's head. When they're out, reporting stops.

What we build: a repeatable process with written documentation anyone on your team can follow.
Planning

No budget, or a budget nobody checks

Decisions about hiring, a new location, or a payer contract get made on gut feel because actuals and plan never sit side by side.

What we build: budget vs. actual reporting and a forecast you can update in an afternoon.

How It Works

A clear scope and a fixed price before any build.

Every engagement starts small. You see how we work and what we'd build before committing to anything larger.

Step 01
Reporting Review
30 minutes · No cost

A call to walk through how your month-end works today: which systems you use, what gets done by hand, and where the numbers stop tying.

Step 02
Diagnostic
One to two weeks · Fixed fee

We map your data sources, document the current process, and find where it breaks. You receive a written build plan with scope, timeline, and a fixed price. The fee is credited toward the build if you go ahead.

Step 03
Build
Typically four to eight weeks

We build the reporting system in your accounts, test it against your closed months, and train your team. Everything is documented and owned by you.

Step 04
Monthly Support
Optional · Flat monthly fee

We keep it running as your business changes: new locations, new payers, new reports, plus analysis when you need it.

Pricing depends on the number of systems and locations involved, and is always agreed in writing before work begins.
