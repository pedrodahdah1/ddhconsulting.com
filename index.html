The situation

The sources never sit in one view.

Finance, claims, operations, and provider data already exist. They do not cross-check themselves, and nobody is told when they drift. Someone still has to go looking. Automation is how that stops.

The close still depends on a workbook

The same exports are pulled and repaired every month. One late file and the package slips.

Deposits, remittances, and the ledger disagree

Cash hits the bank, payers send remittances, and the general ledger shows something else.

Compensation is rebuilt every cycle

Provider incentives or sales commissions are reconstructed by hand, so every payout raises a question.

A direct question takes days

Which site is off, which payer is slow, which provider is an outlier. The answer is still another spreadsheet.

One person knows how reporting works

The process lives in one workbook and one memory. When that person is out, the month stops.

The model no one wants to extend

SQL Server and SSAS still hold the numbers leadership asks about, and every new cut becomes a project.

Month-End Autopilot, Cash Truth, the Commission Engine, and BI Modernization are examples of the automation inside the retainer. They are weighted to what the Diagnostic finds. They are parts of one engagement, not a menu.

Close and reporting automation

Month-End Autopilot

How it works

From a conversation to a monthly cadence.

  5. Monthly cadence

    Leadership reviews

    Reporting on a set rhythm, a review with leadership, and continued work on the systems underneath.
