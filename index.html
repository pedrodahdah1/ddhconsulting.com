The problem

The group runs. The data still sits in pieces.

Every month, on a medical group or a pharmacy group, someone:

  • Repairs the same workbook to close the month.
  • Matches deposits, remittances and the ledger by hand.
  • Rebuilds provider pay from scratch.
  • Waits days for an answer the report should have flagged.

That is time your best people should spend running the group.
