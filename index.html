What DDH delivers

DDH Consulting provides fractional finance and data leadership for healthcare operators. Work is delivered in the client's own accounts, by connecting systems, payers, and the general ledger to a monthly reporting system.

Each month, clients receive:

  1. 01

    Month-end close

    The P&L is tied to the general ledger after close, with the variance explained.

  2. 02

    Rolling forecast

    The budget, the rolling forecast, and the variance, kept current through the year.

  3. 03

    Cash and runway

    Cash on hand, collections, and runway in one view, updated as money arrives.

  4. 04

    Payer economics

    835 remittances matched to deposits and the ledger, with what is still owed by payer. This includes revenue recovery: denials, underpayments, and billing and AR leakage.

  5. 05

    Board pack

    The monthly pack and the board deck, from the same definitions, produced on a fixed schedule.

Power BI is a packaged monthly reporting system: ongoing data maintenance and reporting after close, sitting under the finance-lead offer. DDH is not a Power BI contractor and does not provide staff augmentation.

A scheduled AI briefing is delivered from that model at the time agreed with the client. It can stand alone as a fixed-scope package, or sit inside the monthly reporting retainer.

The retainer

Managed Financial Reporting: a fractional finance lead, on a fixed month.

The monthly reporting retainer, Managed Financial Reporting, is a set weekly commitment and a fixed monthly scope, run in the client's own accounts: month-end close, the rolling forecast, cash and runway, payer economics, the board pack, Power BI reporting, and the scheduled AI briefing.

  • Month-end close. The P&L tied to the general ledger after close, plus variance.
  • Rolling forecast. Budget and forecast brought forward, with the variance explained.
  • Cash and runway. Cash on hand and a view of how long it lasts.
  • Payer economics. Collections, outstanding balances, denials, underpayments, and billing and AR leakage, with remittances tied to the bank and the ledger.
  • Board pack. The monthly pack and the board deck, from the same definitions.
  • Power BI reporting. A packaged monthly system — data maintenance and reporting after close — on the client's Microsoft stack.
  • Scheduled AI briefing. An email briefing, written by AI from the Power BI semantic model, sent from the client's Outlook at the agreed time. Included when the client already has this retainer; also available as a standalone package.

Inside the retainer, DDH builds Month-End Autopilot, Cash Truth, the Commission Engine, and BI Modernization. Those are the systems. The retainer is how clients get them, together with ongoing finance-lead work.

After a six-month minimum, continue month to month, or take a handover: the run book, training, and the client's team runs the system.

Scheduled AI briefing

At an agreed time, a scheduled job reads agreed KPIs from the client's Power BI semantic model and drafts a short email briefing, sent from their Outlook. It is built in their environment, tested until they accept the numbers and the wording, and left running there. It is a fixed-scope package, not hours and not a hire. Production never runs in a chat with an assistant. It can stand alone or sit inside the Managed Financial Reporting retainer.

DDH helps healthcare operators modernize their technology infrastructure, strengthen their enterprise applications, improve operational efficiency and support growth — while meeting HIPAA, their contracts and the rules of their tenant.

The product default is aggregated indicators only, with no patient-level data. Patient-level data is used only with a signed Business Associate Agreement, an explicit need, and an eligible API. The design is HIPAA-eligible with a signed BAA and the correct configuration. A BAA does not replace the client's own compliance program.

Two delivery paths are chosen in a short diagnosis on the Reporting Review call, not as a self-serve purchase.

A

Path A — everything in the client's Microsoft tenant

The query, the AI draft and the email run in Copilot Studio / Power Automate. Data stays in the tenant. This path needs Copilot Credits already in the test phase, not only at go-live: without credits the flow can be designed, but it will not publish and will not run. The no-charge messages that come with Microsoft 365 Copilot do not cover this scheduled trigger. Path A does not go live without Copilot Credits.

B

Path B — enterprise AI API, with a compliance filter in the middle

The query and the outbound email stay in the client's tenant. Only a minimal extract of aggregated KPIs, after a compliance filter, is sent to an enterprise AI API that has a signed BAA. The draft comes back and is sent from their Outlook. DDH does not keep the numbers. Execution logs stay in their tenant. The usual first choice for healthcare clients already on Microsoft is Azure OpenAI in their own Azure subscription. The other eligible options are Claude's HIPAA-ready API with a BAA, or the xAI enterprise API after a BAA, and only on the Zero Data Retention endpoint. A questionnaire is not a signed BAA. Never Claude Free, Pro or Console for patient-level data; never grok.com, the consumer app, or Grok on X. The filter allowlists measures; strips names, medical record numbers, NPIs, claim IDs and addresses; and suppresses small cells that could identify a person. If the filter fails, the AI is not called and an alert is raised. A named person reviews the wording at the start, until the client chooses otherwise. Path B does not go live with patient-level data without a signed BAA and an eligible API.
