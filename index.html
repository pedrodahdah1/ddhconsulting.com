What we build

What we do

We cover data pipelines and a model, Power BI reporting, the close, reconciliation, and automation, in the systems you already use.

  • Data pipelines and data model

    Your EHR or practice management system, billing, bank, and general ledger land in one model. Reports read from that model, and an alert names any file that is late.

  • Power BI and reporting

    The monthly reports and the board pack in Power BI, published after the close, from the same definitions.

  • FP&A and close

    The month-end close, the rolling forecast, and cash, ready a few days after month end. The P&L matches the general ledger, and the variance is explained.

  • Reconciliation and accounts receivable

    Bank deposits, remittance files, and the general ledger, matched every month. A weekly view of accounts receivable past 90 days, underpayments checked against the contract, and a denials list with an owner.

  • AI and automation

    Scheduled reports, alerts when a file is late, and a short briefing email from your Outlook, at a time you set. It uses your Power BI model and is built in your environment.

Your data lead, every month.

Each month, you receive:

  • Data refresh. Source files come in on a schedule, the model refreshes, and an alert names any file that is late.
  • Power BI reports. The monthly reports and the board pack, published from the same definitions.
  • Month-end close. The P&L matches the general ledger after the close, and the variance is explained.
  • Rolling forecast and cash. The budget, the rolling forecast, cash on hand, and how many months of cash you have.
  • Payer mix and margin. Remittances matched to the ledger, with denials and payments missed or underpaid called out.

Power BI holds the monthly reports. We keep the data current and publish the reports after the close.

The scheduled AI briefing is a short email from your Outlook, at a time you set. It uses your Power BI model and is built in your environment.
