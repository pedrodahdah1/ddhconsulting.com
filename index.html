Who We Are

A boutique firm built for healthcare finance.

DDH Consulting is a boutique data architecture and business intelligence firm specializing in healthcare finance. We work with physician groups, management services organizations, specialty pharmacies, and health systems to build the data infrastructure that transforms raw operational data into meaningful financial intelligence.

Our engagements range from custom data warehouse builds and reporting systems to healthcare program analysis and revenue cycle analytics — all designed around the specific operational and financial needs of each client.

What We Do

Four practice areas. One firm.

01

Data Architecture & Engineering

We design and implement end-to-end data infrastructure — from source system integration and pipeline development to data warehouse architecture and semantic modeling. Our solutions are built to scale with your organization and integrate across your existing technology ecosystem.

02

Business Intelligence & Reporting

We translate complex healthcare financial data into clear, actionable dashboards and reporting systems. Whether your team needs executive financial reporting, operational performance visibility, or payer analytics, we deliver BI solutions your finance and operations leadership can rely on daily.

03

Healthcare Revenue Analytics

We develop analytical systems that cross-reference your organization's clinical and financial data against program enrollment records, reimbursement benchmarks, and quality performance data — giving your team a clear, data-driven view of revenue performance across your payer mix and program participation.

04

Ongoing Data Operations

Beyond implementation, we support clients with managed data operations — maintaining pipelines, refreshing intelligence models, and ensuring your reporting infrastructure keeps pace with your organization's growth and evolving data needs.

Our Approach

About

Built from the inside out.

DDH Consulting was founded by Pedro Dahdah, a corporate finance and financial systems professional with over 10 years of experience in FP&A, financial reporting, and data architecture across healthcare, real estate, and multi-unit operations in the U.S. and Latin America.

Pedro currently serves as Manager of Finance Transformation at the University of Miami Health System — one of Florida's largest academic health systems. That role provides direct, working knowledge of the data infrastructure challenges facing complex healthcare organizations: fragmented systems, unreliable reporting, and the gap between what finance leadership needs and what their current tools can deliver.

Before UHealth, Pedro spent two and a half years as a Financial Data Analyst at SMP Pharmacy Solutions, where he led the development of automated Power BI reporting infrastructure that reduced reporting time by 40% and materially improved data accuracy across the organization.

Every engagement is led directly by Pedro. No account managers. No junior staff. No hand-offs after the sale.
