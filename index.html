The problem

The business runs. The reporting still runs on spreadsheets.

Every month, someone on your team:

  • Pulls the same exports and repairs the same workbook to close the month.
  • Matches deposits to payer remittances and the ledger by hand.
  • Rebuilds commissions or provider incentives from scratch.
  • Spends days answering "which site is off?"

That is time your best people should spend running the business.

Who it's for
