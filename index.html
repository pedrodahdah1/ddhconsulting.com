01 · Where the hours go

Most reporting problems start in the same four places.

We see these in practices, surgery centers and pharmacy groups of every size. Count how many sound like your month.

01

The same exports, by hand, every month

Someone pulls files from the EHR, billing and the bank and pastes them into a spreadsheet. It takes days, and when that person is out, the report is late.

What you see One spreadsheet only one person can update

02

Numbers that disagree

Billing shows one revenue figure and the general ledger shows another. The meeting starts with which number is right, and the decision waits.

What you see Two versions of revenue in the same meeting

03

A close that runs into the next month

The month-end close drags into the third week. By the time the P&L, the forecast and cash arrive, they describe a month you can no longer change.

What you see Last month's P&L lands mid-month

04

Money nobody is assigned to chase

Underpayments, denials and claims past 90 days sit in reports nobody owns. Without a name next to each one, the follow-up never starts.

What you see A denials list with no owner
