Not a dashboard project. The finance function your operation never staffed.

DDH is a fractional finance and data lead for healthcare operators, working in the client's own accounts.

Each month, clients receive:

  • Month-end close. The P&L is tied to the general ledger after close, with the variance explained.
  • Rolling forecast. The budget, the rolling forecast, and the variance, kept current through the year.
  • Cash and runway. Cash on hand, collections, and runway in one view.
  • Payer economics. Remittances matched to the ledger, including denials, underpayments, and billing and AR leakage.
  • Board pack. The monthly pack and the board deck, from the same definitions.

Power BI is the monthly reporting system: we keep the data current and produce the reports after close, as part of the finance-lead work.

The scheduled AI briefing is a short email from their Outlook, at a time the client sets, drawn from their Power BI model and built in their environment.
