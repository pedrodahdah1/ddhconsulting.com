What DDH delivers

DDH Consulting provides fractional finance and data leadership for healthcare operators, in the client's own accounts.

Each month, clients receive:

  • Month-end close. The P&L is tied to the general ledger after close, with the variance explained.
  • Rolling forecast. The budget, the rolling forecast, and the variance, kept current through the year.
  • Cash and runway. Cash on hand, collections, and runway in one view.
  • Payer economics. Remittances matched to the ledger, including denials, underpayments, and billing and AR leakage.
  • Board pack. The monthly pack and the board deck, from the same definitions.

Power BI is a packaged monthly reporting system — data maintenance and reporting after close — under the finance-lead offer. A scheduled AI briefing is delivered from that model at the agreed time.

Managed Financial Reporting

The monthly reporting retainer is a set weekly commitment and a fixed monthly scope, run in the client's accounts: the close, the four leadership views, Power BI reporting, and the scheduled AI briefing.

Inside the retainer, DDH builds Month-End Autopilot, Cash Truth, the Commission Engine, and BI Modernization. After a six-month minimum, continue month to month, or take a handover: the run book, training, and the client's team runs the system.

Scheduled AI briefing

At an agreed time, a scheduled job reads agreed KPIs from the client's Power BI semantic model and drafts a short email, sent from their Outlook. It is built in their environment, tested until they accept the numbers and the wording, and left running there. It can stand alone or sit inside the retainer. Production never runs in a chat with an assistant.

DDH helps healthcare operators modernize their technology infrastructure, strengthen their enterprise applications, improve operational efficiency and support growth — while meeting HIPAA, their contracts and the rules of their tenant.

The standard briefing uses aggregated indicators only. The design is HIPAA-eligible with a signed BAA and the correct configuration.

Who it's for

This is a fit if you run an independent or specialty pharmacy, a physician practice, an ASC, or an MSO — or another healthcare operator — and you need a finance lead for the close, the forecast, cash, payer economics, and the board pack. A BI team is still a fit.

Probably not a fit if you want a one-off dashboard with no run after it goes live, or if nobody on your side can own the numbers with us.

DDH typically works with CFOs, COOs, CEOs, and practice administrators.

Method

Every engagement runs on the Cross-Check method. Delivery is on Power BI, Power Automate, Copilot Studio, Microsoft Fabric, and SQL/Azure. Data sources typically include Excel, QuickBooks, and NetSuite. The client owns the code. Most financial reporting uses aggregated indicators; when patient-level data is required, DDH signs a BAA first and uses a design that is HIPAA-eligible with a signed BAA and the correct configuration.
