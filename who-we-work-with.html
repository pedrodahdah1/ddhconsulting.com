Who we work with

Healthcare operators who need their numbers on time.

Most of our work is with finance and operations leaders who run reporting on top of their day job.

Medical practices and groups

You run a practice or a group with several providers. The close depends on your accountant, collections live in the billing system, and provider pay comes from a spreadsheet. We put the close, collections and provider reporting on one model and run it every month.

Typical first fix A monthly P&L and collections report from one source.

Specialty practices and surgery centers

You need case volume, payer mix and margin by procedure, and the numbers from the scheduling system rarely match finance. We connect them so operations and finance read the same figures.

Typical first fix Margin by procedure and payer.

Pharmacy groups

You run several locations and want margin by location, payer and drug class, with reimbursement checked against what you paid. We connect the pharmacy system, the wholesaler invoices and the ledger.

Typical first fix Margin by location and payer.

MSOs and PE-backed groups

You add sites and each one arrives with its own systems and its own reports. We set one reporting standard and a board pack that rolls up cleanly as you grow.

Typical first fix One board pack across all sites.

Teams that already have a BI team

Your analysts build dashboards, and the close and the pipelines still eat their weeks. We take the pipelines, the close and the monthly routine, and your team keeps building on a model that stays current.

Typical first fix Pipelines and definitions your analysts can build on.

We usually work with CFOs, COOs, CEOs, controllers and practice administrators.
